FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer 24/7 emergency call-outs?

Yes — for burst pipes, total power loss and other urgent issues we offer same-day and out-of-hours call-outs across Leeds, Wakefield and surrounding areas.

Are your engineers certified and insured?

All electrical work is carried out by NICEIC-approved engineers and all gas/plumbing work by Gas Safe registered engineers. We carry £5m public liability insurance.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes, we provide a free, no-obligation written quote for all non-emergency work before anything is booked in.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Leeds and cover all of West Yorkshire, including Wakefield, Bradford, Huddersfield and surrounding towns.

Do you work on commercial premises?

Yes — we run planned maintenance contracts for schools, retail units, offices and leisure sites alongside our residential work.
