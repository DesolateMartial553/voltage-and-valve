For landlords & agents

Landlord certificates, sorted in one visit

Gas and electrical paperwork for let properties across Leeds and West Yorkshire — booked around your tenants, with the certificate emailed the same day.

CP12 gas safety record

from £40

The annual check required for every let property with a gas appliance. We test the boiler, hob and fire, then issue your CP12 for you to hand to tenants.

EICR electrical report

from £150

A full inspection of wiring and consumer unit, with clear pass/fail coding and a quote for any remedial work. Needed at least every five years for rented homes.
